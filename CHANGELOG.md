### Fixed
- **Strategy Lab / Backtest:** Iron Condor (and any preset) on an expiry missing calls or puts now reports a clear reason (`NO_CALL_CONTRACTS`, `NO_PUT_CONTRACTS`, `EMPTY_CHAIN`) instead of a raw Python error.
